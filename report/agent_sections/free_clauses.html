The overwhelming pattern confirms Palavecino was the rule, not the exception: across every high-value "free transfer" we checked, the vast majority were plain contract expiries with zero compensation to the selling club. But 8 real exceptions surfaced — cases where Transfermarkt's "free transfer" tag hid genuine money or a real clause:

The rest — the overwhelming majority of the sample — checked out exactly like Palavecino: a contract ran to zero, and the selling club got nothing, no matter how much the player's value had grown. A handful of others were "released" rather than "expired" (the club or player ended the deal early), but even those carried no compensation in almost every case we could verify. Two entries that appeared in an earlier draft of this table — a claimed Sebastián Pérez swap negotiation and a claimed Francisco Arancibia release indemnity — were retracted after a full revalidation pass found their source articles never actually said what was originally recorded.
