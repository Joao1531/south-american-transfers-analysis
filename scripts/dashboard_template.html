Methodology & limitations

    __UNIVERSE_LI__
  • Each transfer is a single record; when both clubs involved are inside the universe above, the same deal was cross-matched from both clubs' pages to avoid double counting.
  • "Market value" is Transfermarkt's own market-value estimate at the time of the transfer, where available — it is not the fee actually paid (that's the "Fee" column).
  • "What happened to this player next" (visible when a row is expanded) shows the player's subsequent moves — including a later sale by the club that signed him, which matters for any sell-on percentage tied to the original deal.
    • __SELLON_LI__
  • Not included: per-season match/goal/assist statistics — Transfermarkt moved those pages to JS-only components with no stable API, which made reliable scraping at the scale of ~1,340 players impractical.
    • __CMF_LI__
  • Fee and market-value figures are sourced from Transfermarkt in EUR and converted to USD at a flat rate of €1 = $1.08 for display.__SELLON_FEE_NOTE__
